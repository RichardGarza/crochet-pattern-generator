except where noted.

## Deviations from the spec, with reasons

1. **ZIP reading (§3.7.2 "fflate `unzipSync` (filtered)").** A small central-directory reader + fflate `inflateSync`
   per entry into a buffer of the declared size (exactly what `unzipSync` does inside). Reason: the limits are
   checked on the directory before anything is inflated, entry times are needed for the tie-break (and the
   captured archive's DOS times are UTC wall time, its true time is in the UT extra field, which `unzipSync` does
   not expose), and duplicate names are kept apart.
2. **Pasted text that is an HTML page** goes through the HTML ladder (carrier `html` / `standalone-html`) instead of
   the text strategy: entity-escaped attributes and the standalone template are only readable that way.
3. **Radians** are decided in the dialect step on the rotations as written, before parent rotations are composed;
   composing degrees and converting afterwards is wrong for nested parts. The chip still sorts in the radians slot.
4. **Grounding** ignores a shift below 1e-6 in (rounding noise), so a canonical model imports as itself.
5. **Additions beyond the table of §3.7.3**, each logged: `"units": "cm" | "mm" | "m"` converts the whole spec;
   `axes.up: "+Z"` turns the model −90° about X (an `axes` chip; the flat-base case of §3.7.6 is only *offered*,
   as an `axes` chip with `data.offer = true`); a palette object `{ id: "#hex" }` (the reverse of the dialect),
   a palette of plain hexes, colors written as `0xRRGGBB` or `[r, g, b]`; `position` / `rotationDeg` as
   `{ x, y, z }`; `rotation` read as `rotationDeg` (the radians rule decides the unit); three.js type names
   (`SphereGeometry`, `TorusGeometry` with `radius` / `tube` / `arc` in radians); `cube` → box; `openEnded` →
   `open: 'both'`; model `title` → `name`; top-level `note`, `comment`, `comments` kept in `assumptions` like
   `notes`; feature kind aliases (`eye` → `safety_eye`, `blush` → `cheek`, …).
6. **Canonical capsules shorter than their caps** (`length < 2r`) are read as the straight section (a
   `dims-clamped` chip) instead of being clamped to `2r`: that is how three.js and the observed dialect write them.
7. **`W_MIN_FEATURE` is `info`**, not a warning: thin parts are normal (nose, foot pads, ear linings) and §2.10.1
   turns them into embroidery, regions or appliqués; parts marked `safety_eye` / `embroidery` / `skip`, flat and
   mesh parts are not reported.
8. **Failure results** carry `dialect: 'canonical-1'` (the field is required and has no "none").
9. **Several dropped files** report the carrier of the input whose spec was chosen; candidate ids are the entry
   path in a zip, `k:path` across several inputs (k = 1-based input index).
10. **Work budgets** (not in the spec): the E4 brace scan and the text search share one budget per text or page
    (4 × its length in characters, at least 4 MB; 64 parse attempts; 512 braces looked at per match), brace
    matches are memoized, at most 16 fences and 64 marker blocks are tried; raw palettes are read up to 256 entries
    and raw parts up to 1000 (the limits step then keeps 16 / 60). More than 20 chips of one code in one step are
    summed up in one more chip (`… and N more like these`). Hostile inputs that took minutes now take
    milliseconds (`review.test.ts`).
11. **A `.json` file or a whole pasted text is a spec only when its `schema` is `crochet-model`** (§3.7.2); an
    HTML block found by E2 (`#crochet-model`, …) may omit `schema`.
12. **Units, beyond the ratio rule:** with a declared unit the geometry is converted once and the finished height is
    kept as inches when it already matches the converted model; a stated height outside 0–60 in is ignored (and
    the measured one recorded); a model measured above 60 in is scaled to 60 (`limits`); after dims are clamped the
    height is measured again.
13. **GLB ladder step 1** (`extras.crochetModel`) is in T7.1 so archive GLBs are candidates; everything else of
    §3.7.5 answers `E_IMPORT_UNSUPPORTED` with what to drop instead.

## Independent review

Findings and what was done:

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|

## Deviations (T7.2)

1. **PLY is read by our own reader**, not three's `PLYLoader`: the loader converts 8-bit colors to linear and stores them
   back in 8 bits (`#C81E28` came back `#C81C28`). STL uses three's `STLLoader` as specified.
2. **Without `fitPart` (still a T3 stub) geometry parts are `mesh` parts** with their buffers in `ImportResult.meshes`
   (vertices about the bbox center, labels into the model palette) and a `W_IMPORT_PARSE` warning saying so. Even so
   the teddy OBJ gets the canonical attach tree and mirror pairs (the inscribed-ellipsoid SDF of mesh parts suffices).
3. **Units beyond §3.7.5:** an inches reading above 60 in with no expected height tries mm, then cm (reason `spec`,
   confirmed) — a 251 mm STL otherwise failed validation. The plain-inches default reports reason `spec` (the union
   has no "default"). GLB step 3 ignores `ctx.units` (the ratio is exact).
4. **GLB step 2** uses the model group's name as the model name (builder: `group.name = spec.name`); step 3 does not.
5. **Plain `.tar` and single gzipped files** (`teddy.obj.gz`, `spec.json.gz`) are read; a gzip in a gzip is refused.
   A zip entry or gzip that inflates past its declared size is now refused (`E_IMPORT_TOO_LARGE`) instead of being
   truncated (T7.1 behaviour): inflation is streamed and stops there, so a lying header costs milliseconds.
8. **GLB step 4 colors** are collected per vertex (`COLOR_0`, else each primitive's material) and reduced to ≤ 16 yarn
   colors for the whole file; material names name the colors they match. Strips and fans are read as triangles.
9. **GLB step 3** consults `ctx.units` (the user's answer wins over the measured ratio) and sets `confirm` when the
   parts' sizes disagree with their meshes by more than 5%.
10. **`expectedHeightIn` outside 0.1–60 in is ignored** (an absurd value overflowed Float32 vertex buffers).
6. **Derived OBJ, STL and PLY are gzipped** (`.obj.gz`, `.mm.stl.gz`, `.ply.gz`) like the captured OBJ; the PLY (meters,
   colors) and STL (mm), the painted GLB and the two handoff bundles are extra fixtures. The page ran three 0.186.1.
7. **`acceptImport` takes a `MeshCodec`** because the asset format of a `ColoredMesh` is not defined yet (request 1).

## Requests for integration (T7.2)

1. A shared `ColoredMesh` asset codec (mime + header, like `CPGL`) for `threeD.meshAssets`: T3, T5, T6 and T7 need the
   same one. Until then `acceptImport` refuses mesh parts without a caller-supplied codec.
2. §3.7.5: add the "beyond 60 in → mm, then cm" fallback (deviation 3), and say which `reason` a plain-inches reading
   reports (`spec` here), or add a `'default'` member to `UnitsDecision.reason`.
3. §3.7.2: add plain `.tar` and single `.gz` files to the detection table; §3.7.5: PLY by our own reader (deviation 1).
