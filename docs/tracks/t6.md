## Deviations (with reasons)

1. **Round sizes are shown as diameters** ("Width" = 2·rx, "Diameter" = 2·r, "Ring diameter", "Tube thickness"):
   crocheters measure across a piece. The model keeps radii (§3.5.1); the fields convert.
2. **Resize (gizmo) keeps the part's CENTER fixed** (the gizmo's pivot; a lathe's origin moves), while Parameters
   (inspector fields) keep the part's `position` (its origin, §0.1: a lathe's base). §4.2 does not say which.
3. **"Attached parts follow" toggle** (on by default) next to the tools; ⌥ inverts it for one drag. §4.2 has only
   ⌥; the toggle makes the "part alone" edit reachable from the keyboard and the inspector's fields (which have no
   drag to hold ⌥ on). Inspector position / rotation edits follow the same rule as the gizmo.
4. **Snapping while resizing** rounds the dims that changed to the 0.05 in grid (§4.2 names 0.05 in / 5° for the
   gizmos without saying how Scale snaps).
5. **Resize of mesh parts is disabled** with a reason (baking into the vertex buffer needs the mesh-asset
   plumbing of T6.4); the viewport gets `meshes = {}` until mesh assets are decoded (T6.4), so a mesh part is drawn
   as the builder's `bboxIn` ellipsoid.
6. **`revision` + 1 per editor history step** (§3.5.1 "+1 on every edit"); a drag bumps it once.
7. **Mirror-linked twins do not follow drags yet** — "linked edits propagate" belongs to the Mirror tool (T6.2).
8. **Sample teddy** in the empty state ("Try the sample teddy"), as the brief asks for the fixture as the demo
   model; it enters as a model revision (`source: 'import'`, one undo step).
9. **The viewport talks to `editorStore` and the slice directly**: the frozen `ViewportProps` has no gizmo or
   camera callbacks; a stub `Viewport` simply ignores them.
10. **Re-anchoring of the Inspector's size SLIDER is throttled like the gizmo** (`beginResizeGesture`, 10 Hz): the
    slider is a drag too.
11. **Dims edits always leave a valid model** (§3.5.2), also at extremes: a capsule is never shorter than two caps of
    the smallest radius (0.1 in); a lathe profile's largest radius and its height are kept in [0.05, 48] by scaling
    the radii / heights (shape kept); a polygon flat's `points` follow its width and height, and other flat shapes
    drop `points`; a sphere whose snapped radii come out equal stays a sphere. Lathe radii are not snapped.
12. **Escape during a gizmo drag cancels it** (no history step, the part goes back); Escape otherwise clears the
    selection. Q/W/E/R do nothing on a read-only project.
13. **A hint on the view** when the active tool cannot act ("Select a part to move it", or why a sculpted part
    cannot be resized).

### T6.2 deviations

14. **Mirror on an unpaired part without a side token** keeps the part's id and makes `<id>_r` (§4.2 speaks of
    `_l` → `_r` only); its label becomes "<name> mirrored" ("Left …" → "Right …" when the label names a side). Mirror
    is refused for mesh parts (baking a mirrored vertex buffer needs the T6.4 mesh plumbing) and for parts on the
    middle line (|center x| < 0.05 in: the image would land on itself).
15. **Linked edits copy** shape, place, color, stuffing, flatten, regions, paint and crochet hints — not the id,
    label, attach, notes or other `x-*` keys; only when exactly one side of the pair changed (a move of the pair's
    common parent moves both and rewrites neither). A pair that was not an exact mirror snaps to one on its first
    linked edit.
16. **Deleting the root** is allowed: the largest part left without a parent (by volume) becomes the root and the
    others hang from it (§4.2 only says "children re-attach to the deleted part's parent"). Deleting every part is
    refused. Delete also stores a model revision (§4.4 "destructive actions … create a persisted model revision").
17. **Duplicate** copies the selected parts only (not their subtrees), "<name> copy", `mirrorOf` dropped; a copy of
    the root hangs from the root, so the model stays one tree.
18. **Add part** turns round shapes so their length points along the clicked normal and flat pieces / rings so their
    face does (a horn sticks out, a patch lies on the surface); the default size is 0.35 × the parent's middle
    extent (0.3–3 in). §4.2 says only where it goes.
19. **Attach does not move the part**: a re-parented part far from its new parent shows the `W_GAP` warning; open end
    "Neither" = `openEnd: 'none'`; method "Sewn on (default)" = no `method` key. Choosing a top/bottom open end also
    deletes `x-cpg-proximal` (redundant then).
20. **Yarn & size, finished size**: the toy ghost height of §2.10.10 needs the live pattern loop (T6.3), so the panel
    shows the model's height (pre-model: the build target) with the gauge band `× (1 ± tol)`, widened by the hook
    exponent range when the hook is not the recommended one and nothing was measured (§2.2.2). **Scale model to
    height** is already in the panel (it is the panel's height field, §4.5): uniform `scaleModel`, one step, a new
    revision; refused for models with mesh parts (their buffers come with T6.4) and when a dim would pass 48 in.
21. **Pre-filling from `model.yarn`** happens in the `post-import` context when the import created the project
    (origin `claude-design`) and the gauge is still the untouched default; otherwise the panel offers "Use it" (never
    overwriting a user-set gauge, §3.7.7). T7 may pre-fill itself with `yarnFromModel` / `applyModelYarn`; the panel
    then finds the gauge already set and does nothing.
22. **Spiral lean calibration** asks which side of the ruler the marker is on (left / right, seen from the outside)
    instead of "against / with your working direction"; the side is mapped with the crocheter's hand
    (`ami.hand`: right-handed + right of the ruler = against = positive, §2.11.2).
23. **Pre-model without `threeD.recon`**: the height field is replaced by a sentence; the panel never invents
    `ReconSettings` (T3 owns their defaults).
24. **Inspector pages**: on the Shape tab the panel lives in the inspector's second page ("Part | Yarn & size");
    picking a part switches back to Part.

## Known limits

1. `TransformControls` points an axis arrow toward the camera, so in the Front view a part above the camera shows
   its Y arrow pointing down (three.js behavior).
2. Only Escape interrupts a gizmo drag; `TransformControls` keeps its own drag state until the pointer is released.

measured contrast in the running app. Findings and outcome:

| Finding | Severity | Outcome |

| `ContactShadows` remounted on every edit (keyed); drei never frees its render targets: +56 textures/framebuffers per drag | blocker | always mounted, size in powers of two, off = hidden and not rendered; measured: 30 resizes → live textures 13 → 13, framebuffers 14 → 14 |
| A gizmo Move rewrote Euler angles (deg → rad → deg noise sent it down the rotation path: `[0,180,0]` → `[-180,0,-180]`) | major | rotations compared as matrices (1e-9); test with three's radian noise |
